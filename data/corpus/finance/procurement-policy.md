# Procurement and Vendor Approval Policy

## Approval Thresholds
Purchases up to INR 1 lakh need approval from the budget owner. Purchases between INR 1 lakh and INR 25 lakh need approval from the budget owner and the Finance Controller. Purchases above INR 25 lakh need CFO approval and at least 3 competitive quotes.

## Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must complete KYC, provide a GST registration certificate and PAN, and pass a security review if they will access company or customer data. Vendor onboarding typically takes 10 business days.

## Payment Terms
Standard vendor payment terms are net 45 days from invoice receipt. MSME-registered vendors are paid within 30 days as required by law.
