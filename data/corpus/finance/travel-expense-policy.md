# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking Travel
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days in advance for domestic trips and 21 days in advance for international trips. Economy class is the default for all flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 6 hours and requires VP approval.

## Hotel Limits
Hotel limits per night are INR 9,000 in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and INR 6,000 in all other Indian cities. For international travel, the limit is USD 220 per night.

## Daily Allowance
The daily meal allowance is INR 2,000 for domestic travel and USD 75 for international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Submitting Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the trip ending, with itemised receipts for every expense above INR 500. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment is capped at INR 4,000 per attendee and requires the names and companies of all attendees on the claim.
