# Data Retention Policy

## Retention Periods
Payment transaction records are retained for 10 years to meet RBI and anti-money-laundering requirements. Customer KYC documents are retained for 5 years after the customer relationship ends. Application logs are retained for 180 days, and security audit logs for 2 years.

## Deletion Requests
Customer requests to delete personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act must be completed within 30 days, except for data the company is legally required to retain.

## Backups
Database backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days.
