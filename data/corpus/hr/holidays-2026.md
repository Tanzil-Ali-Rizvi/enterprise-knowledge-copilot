# Holiday Calendar 2026

Northwind Pay offices observe 12 fixed holidays and 2 floating holidays in 2026.

## Fixed Holidays
Republic Day (26 January), Holi (4 March), Eid al-Fitr (21 March), Good Friday (3 April), Labour Day (1 May), Independence Day (15 August), Ganesh Chaturthi (14 September), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Dussehra (20 October), Diwali (8 November), Guru Nanak Jayanti (24 November) and Christmas (25 December).

## Floating Holidays
Each employee may choose 2 floating holidays from the optional list, such as Pongal, Onam, Eid al-Adha or Durga Puja. Floating holidays must be applied for in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance.
