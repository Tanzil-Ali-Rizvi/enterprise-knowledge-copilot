# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Pay (a fictional company used for this demo).

## Earned Leave
Full-time employees accrue 24 days of earned leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 15 unused earned leave days can be carried forward into the next year. Earned leave beyond the carry-forward limit lapses on 31 December. Earned leave requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive parents of a child under 12 months receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## Leave Encashment
On resignation, unused earned leave of up to 30 days is encashed at the basic salary rate.
