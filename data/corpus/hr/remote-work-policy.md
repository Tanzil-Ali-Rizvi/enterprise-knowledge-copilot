# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Office Attendance
Northwind Pay follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday as mandatory team anchor days. Team leads may approve a different schedule for roles that are primarily customer-facing.

## Fully Remote Exceptions
Fully remote work requires approval from both the department head and HR, and is reviewed every 6 months. Employees may work from another city in India for up to 30 days per year without approval. Working from outside India requires prior written approval from HR and Legal because of tax and data-protection obligations.

## Home Office Allowance
Employees receive a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, plus a monthly internet reimbursement of up to INR 1,500. Claims are submitted through the expense tool with the original invoice.

## Equipment and Security
Company laptops must not be used on public Wi-Fi without the company VPN. Employees must not let family members use company devices.
