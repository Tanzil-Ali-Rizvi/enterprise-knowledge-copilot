# Compensation Bands 2026

Confidential. Visible to HR and the executive team only.

## Engineering Bands
Software Engineer (L3) base salary band is INR 18 lakh to 28 lakh per year. Senior Software Engineer (L4) band is INR 30 lakh to 45 lakh per year. Staff Engineer (L5) band is INR 48 lakh to 70 lakh per year.

## Annual Increments
The 2026 merit increase budget is 9% of total base payroll. Employees rated "exceeds expectations" are eligible for increments of 12% to 18%. Increments are effective from 1 April.

## Bonus
The annual performance bonus target is 10% of base salary for L3 and L4, and 15% for L5 and above, paid in June.
