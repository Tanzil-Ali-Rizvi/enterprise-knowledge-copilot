# Q3 FY2026 Board Summary

Strictly confidential. Executive team only.

## Financial Performance
Q3 revenue was INR 142 crore, up 31% year over year. EBITDA was INR 18 crore, an EBITDA margin of 12.7%. Cash runway is 26 months at the current burn rate.

## Business Highlights
Total payment volume crossed INR 9,800 crore in the quarter. UPI AutoPay grew to 2,100 active merchants. Customer churn fell to 1.4% per month.

## Risks
The main risks are the pending RBI payment aggregator licence renewal and rising fraud losses in the card-not-present segment.

## Next Quarter Priorities
Launch the credit line on UPI product, complete the SOC 2 Type II audit, and hire a Chief Risk Officer.
