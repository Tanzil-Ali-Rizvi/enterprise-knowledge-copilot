# Office Wi-Fi and Printing Guide

## Connecting to Wi-Fi
Employees connect to the "NW-Corp" network using their company SSO credentials. Guests use the "NW-Guest" network with a daily password shown at reception. Personal phones should use NW-Guest, not NW-Corp.

## Printing
Printers are on every floor. Send jobs to "NW-Print" and release them at any printer by tapping your ID badge. Colour printing is limited to 200 pages per month.
