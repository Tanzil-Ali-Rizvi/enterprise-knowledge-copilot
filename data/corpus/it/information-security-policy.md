# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be stored only in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, cloud consoles and source control. Hardware security keys are required for engineers with production access.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer payment data, Aadhaar numbers and card numbers are Restricted. Restricted data must never be pasted into external AI tools, personal email or chat apps.

## Reporting Phishing
Report suspected phishing emails using the "Report Phishing" button in the mail client or by forwarding them to security@northwind-pay.example. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour so the device can be remotely wiped. Call the IT helpdesk on extension 4357 (H-E-L-P).

## Software Installation
Only software from the approved catalogue in the self-service portal may be installed. Requests for new software are reviewed by IT Security within 5 business days.
